reading

What I'm reading in machine hearing, and why.

Reading is where most of my research starts. This page tracks the papers closest to my work: spatial audio understanding, self-supervised audio and speech representation learning, and audio-language models. Keeping the list public keeps me honest about reading what I save, and lets collaborators, students and anyone curious see what I’m thinking about.

Know a paper that belongs here? Send it to me.
